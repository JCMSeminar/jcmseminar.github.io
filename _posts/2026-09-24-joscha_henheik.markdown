# September 24st, 2026: Joscha Henheik

On Thursday, September 24st, 2026 from 13:00 to 13:45 we will host a seminar by **Joscha Henheik** from **University of Oxford**.

## Title
The Eigenstate Thermalisation Hypothesis in Random Matrix Theory: Anomalous Rates at Spectral Singularities

## Abstract 

In this talk, I provide an introduction to the Eigenstate Thermalisation Hypothesis (ETH) in the context of Random Matrix Theory (RMT) and discuss our most recent findings, which invalidate the inverse-density scaling of the ETH fluctuations, as put forward by Feingold and Peres in the 1980s and later incorporated into the celebrated Srednicki ansatz.

Based on joint work with G. Cipolloni, L. Erdos, and V. Riabov.
